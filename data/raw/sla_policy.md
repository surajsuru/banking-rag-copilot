# Service Level and Support Policy

Version 5.0 | Owner: Service Management | Access: Restricted

| Severity | Acknowledge | Specialist | Update cadence |
|---|---:|---:|---:|
| Sev-1 | 5 min | 10 min | 15 min |
| Sev-2 | 15 min | 30 min | 30 min |
| Sev-3 | 60 min | 4 hours | 4 hours |
| Sev-4 | 1 business day | 2 business days | As agreed |

A Sev-1 payment incident means broad inability to complete a critical payment path, not just one failed customer transaction.

A support response target is not a transaction-processing timeout. The 5-minute Sev-1 acknowledgement target does not mean a payment must complete within 5 minutes.

RAG test: questions about response targets should retrieve this file; questions about transaction timeouts should retrieve transaction_processing.md or release notes.
